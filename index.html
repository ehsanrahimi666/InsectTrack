InsectTrack demonstration videos

Twenty videos of bees, wasps, hornets, ants, flies and a butterfly, analysed with the R package InsectTrack. Every insect has its own colour and identity number. Each card plays the tracked video and links to the report, the tables and the figures of the analysis.
